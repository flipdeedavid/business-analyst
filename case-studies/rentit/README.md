# RentIt

## Building Trust Into P2P Equipment Rental

**Role:** Product Manager / Business Analyst  
**Market:** Nigeria

### Problem

People already rent equipment through fragmented channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. This can make trust, deposits, availability, equipment condition and dispute handling difficult.

### Users

**Renters:** individuals, SMEs, creatives, artisans, event planners, photographers/videographers and contractors.

**Owners:** people and businesses with idle or underutilised equipment.

### Equipment

Cameras, generators, drones, projectors, power tools, speakers, tents, catering equipment and construction tools.

### Research

The research framework included stakeholder identification, personas, surveys, interviews, competitor research, pain-point analysis, hypotheses and research findings.

### Core Journey

**Discover/Search → Verify → Select → Book → Pay → Handover/Collect → Rent → Return → Inspect → Resolve Deposit/Dispute → Review**

Owner:

**Register → Verify Identity → List → Receive Booking → Complete Rental → Receive Payment**

### MVP

- KYC / identity verification
- Listings and search
- Booking
- Payment and deposit
- Rental agreement
- Condition Passport
- Handover
- Return inspection
- Dispute resolution
- Reviews

### Broader Roadmap

Delivery, messaging, business accounts and analytics.

Future: AI, insurance, financing and IoT.

### Artefacts

Stakeholder map | Personas | Research plan | Interview questions | Survey | Customer journeys | Process flows | PRD | User stories | Gherkin | Jira backlog | MVP/MMP definition | Executive presentation

### Outcome

A structured product definition was developed from research through MVP scope and delivery-ready requirements.

No adoption, revenue or rental-volume metric is claimed because those outcomes have not been confirmed.

### Learning

Trust is the product in a peer-to-peer marketplace. Verification, condition evidence, deposits, handover and dispute handling must work together end-to-end.
