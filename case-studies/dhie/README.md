# DHIE — David's Health Information Exchange

## Connecting Health Information Around the Patient

**Role:** Founder & Chief Product Manager  
**Market:** Nigeria

### Problem

Patient information can be fragmented across disconnected healthcare systems, making coordinated care and information access more difficult.

DHIE explores a health information exchange that connects relevant health information while preserving privacy, security and appropriate access.

### Users & Stakeholders

Clinicians | Healthcare facilities | Laboratories | Pharmacies | Health information managers | Patients | Administrators

### Product Principles

- Patient-centred
- Privacy and security
- Interoperability
- Evidence before assumptions
- Collaboration
- Responsible AI

### Product Direction

The concept includes:

- Patient search
- Consolidated clinical summary
- Medication and allergy information
- Laboratory results
- Referrals
- Consent
- Authentication
- Role-based access
- Audit trails

### AI Approach

The product direction favours **human-in-the-loop AI agents** that support healthcare professionals rather than replacing clinicians.

### Delivery

The product was structured through an MVP and release-based approach, including an Advanced Clinical Exchange direction.

### Outcome

A structured product concept, MVP direction, requirements and roadmap were developed for a Nigerian health information exchange.

Pilot validation, production adoption and quantified clinical/business outcomes remain future validation areas.

### Learning

In healthcare products, interoperability and governance are product requirements, not technical details to solve later. Trust, consent, security and access control must be designed alongside the experience.
