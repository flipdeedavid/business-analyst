# Tide Care Home

## Problem Validation → Research → Product Thinking → Solution

**Role:** Business Analyst | Product Owner | Product Manager

**Live product:** [Tide Care Home](https://tide-home-henna.vercel.app/)

### Problem

The opportunity was to shape a practical digital experience around care-home users, workflows and operational needs rather than starting with features alone.

### My Approach

**Discovery → workflow analysis → requirements → user stories → acceptance criteria → backlog → product solution**

I focused on understanding users and stakeholders, translating needs into requirements and connecting those requirements to a usable product experience.

### Artefacts

- Requirements documentation
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Workflow/process thinking
- Backlog structure
- Product solution concept

### Outcome

A live product concept demonstrating the translation of business and user needs into a digital experience.

No quantitative business or user outcome is claimed because a measured production outcome has not been confirmed.

### Learning

I would bring usability validation and analytics planning earlier so product decisions can be tested continuously.
