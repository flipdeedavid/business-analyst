# MentorMarche / Mentor Partners

## Exploring a Mentorship Marketplace and Digital Learning Ecosystem

**Role:** Product Manager / Business Analyst

### Problem

Mentor Partners provides digital and technology education, training and mentorship. The opportunity was to explore how a stronger digital product ecosystem could improve discovery, onboarding, learning, mentoring and scalability.

### Existing Service Model

**Student initiates training request → Mentor Partners onboards student → trainer/mentor is assigned**

### Target Segments

- People transitioning into tech
- New hires seeking job support
- People retraining after another provider

### Research

The research framework covered:

- Research objectives
- Research questions
- Mentee personas
- Mentor research
- Interviews
- Surveys
- Competitor research
- Competitor comparison
- Hypotheses
- Assumptions and risks
- Research success criteria

### Product Opportunity

The LMS + CRM direction addresses:

- Course materials distributed through email
- Manual grading and attendance
- Scattered progress, test, feedback and payment information
- Poor mobile certificate / next-lesson experience
- Scaling challenges

### Product Thinking

**Problem → Evidence → Segment → Opportunity → Hypothesis → MVP → Validation → Roadmap**

### Outcome

A structured product research and strategy foundation was developed for MentorMarche and the wider digital learning ecosystem.

The next step is validation with learners, mentors and business stakeholders before treating proposed features as validated demand.

### Learning

For a service business considering platform transformation, the key question is not “What features should we build?” but “Which repeated pain creates enough value to justify productisation?”
