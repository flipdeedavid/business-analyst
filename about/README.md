# 01 — About Me

## My Professional Story

I'm a **Product Manager, Business Analyst, Product Owner and Scrum Master** who enjoys working where a problem is still ambiguous and the path to a solution is not yet obvious.

My approach combines **product discovery, business analysis, requirements management, Agile delivery and stakeholder management**. I translate business and user problems into structured product decisions, clear requirements and actionable delivery plans.

### My Working Style

- Structured and evidence-oriented
- Collaborative with cross-functional teams
- Comfortable working through ambiguity
- Focused on clear requirements and trade-offs
- User-centred without losing sight of business goals

### What I Care About

Strong product work is not about producing the most documents or features. It is about making better decisions with the information available, validating assumptions, communicating trade-offs and learning from outcomes.
