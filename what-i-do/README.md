# 02 — What I Do

## Product Management

Problem framing, discovery, product direction, MVP definition, prioritisation, roadmaps and product metrics.

## Business Analysis

Requirements elicitation, process analysis, stakeholder analysis, gap analysis, use cases and requirements traceability.

## Product Ownership

Backlog management, user stories, acceptance criteria, refinement, prioritisation and release planning.

## Agile Delivery

Scrum practices, iterative delivery, sprint planning, collaboration, dependency management and continuous improvement.

## Requirements Management

BRD, PRD, FRD, SRS, functional/non-functional requirements, Gherkin acceptance criteria and scope definition.

## Process Improvement

AS-IS/TO-BE mapping, workflow analysis, gap analysis, root-cause thinking and improvement recommendations.

## Stakeholder Management

Stakeholder maps, RACI, workshops, interviews, communication and cross-functional alignment.
